Mission number 9: Title: A Sense of Priorities. Subtitle: Today's bomb comes with unusual instructions. A formidable math scientist has tinkered with the device and several of its wires must be cut in a specific order. Just remember your training and everything will be okay. Set up:

* Specifically for this setup you have to shuffle the number cards, draw 3 of them, and set them face up in a line on the table.
* We place the sequence card, which is basically a card with an arrow going from the left to the right, above the leftmost card.
* The 2-player setup is 2 red, 4 yellow.
The special rule is that some wires must be cut in order, specifically the 3 number cards. If they are A, B, and C from left to right, you cannot cut any B wires until you have cut at least 2 A wires. Similarly for C, you cannot cut C until you have cut at least 2 A and B wires. The rest of the wires can be cut in any order. Once the 2 A wires have been cut, you can flip the A card face down and shift the sequence card to the next number card. Similarly do so after 2 B wires have been cut and after 2 C wires have been cut. The special rules no longer apply.
