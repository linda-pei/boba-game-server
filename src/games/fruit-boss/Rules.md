# Components
56 Fruit Cards in 7 suits - Plum, Apple, Orange, Tomato, Watermelon, Lemon, Pear. Three 1's, two 2's, one 3, 4, 5 of each.
3 Maneki-Neko (Cat) cards
3 Wild star fruits
5 stalls (used in center marketplace)

# Setup
2 player - Remove tomato as suit
1. Shuffle the deck. Random player goes first. Subsequent rounds - P1 rotates.
2. Deal 5 Cards to ea player
3. Lay 5 cards face up in a row in center on top of stalls (forming a marketplace)
4. Remaining cards form a draw deck.

# Gameplay
You may perform up to 2 actions in your turn. Thes can be the same action or you can choose to end your turn after one action. Finish your turn by drawing up to 5. In rare case where you are unable to perform any actions - discard your entire hand, shuffle the draw deck and draw a new hand.
## Market Refills
If at any point there is only 1 occupied stall or 0, immediately draw from deck to refill empty stalls with single card. This can happen during your card or after it.
## Actions
### A - Add fruit to market
Play 1 or more fruit cards of the same suit from your hand into the market stalls as a single action. You can increase the value of an existing stack or start a new one. A stack's value = sum of the values on each fruit card in the stack.
* Cards playerd into stack must match the suit of cards already in stall
* If stall is empty, you can play any type of fruit and start a new stack

MANDATORY: If the sum of the stack you added to exceeds that of an adjacent stack of 2+ cards, you collect that lesser stack into your private collection. If both adjacent are collectible, you choose. The stack goes into your private collection to be scored at end of round.

Tip: You may play into the same stack twice as 2 different actions.
### B - Combine stack
You may combine a stack of fruit already in the market with another stack of the same suit.

MANDATORY: Same rule as A for the new combined stack

### C - Slide stack
You may slide a stack across the market to an empty stall as long as there is room to do so. You may slide through multiple empty stalls as a single action. You cannot jump over an occumpied stall and you many not collect stacks in this way.

### D - Play a maneki-neko
It will eat one card of any value in the market, removing it for end of this result. Put the maneki-neko and the card it ate in the discard pile.
* If a maneki-neko ends up in the market, you may capture it in the same way as a fruit stack. They hav ea value of 0 and can be collected as a single card from the market. Instead of adding the card to your portfolio, immediately place it into your hand.
## Wild star fruit
Star Fruits count as wild and whichever fruit they are stacked with both in the market/portfolio. They can be played from your hand with other fruits, collected by themselves, or combined with other fruit in the market. If a stack of only start fruit is collected from the market, they must be immediately applied to a previously collected set of your choice in your portfolio. If your portfolio is empty, they will be assigned to you rfirst collection of cards
## Toppling
If the cards you place would bring the total # of cards in stack to 5+, stack immediately topples -> cards in the toppled stack and one adjacent stack are discared from the market from this around. 
* Toppling stuck MUST topple an adjacent stack if one is available. If two are, you choose the direction.
* You can never collect a stack if the cards you played cause it to topple in your current action.

## Round End
After final card in draw deck is drawn, fire sale is triggered. From this point on, players are allowed to collect single cards from the market while completing actions instead of normal min of 2. Once 1 player runs out of cards, all other players get one more turn.. Round is over.

### Scoring
Players group all simialr fruit in their private collection into stacks and tally the score using below criteria. Player with most points is winner.
1. To score a fruit stack, take the highest value in that stack and multiply it by num of cards in that stack.
2. If star fruit in stack, countas as fruit of that type.
3. You may score only 3 unique fruit stacks, all other unique re scored against you (ie negative points in the same scoring system as (1+2))
4. Fruit cards left in your hand will count as wasted resources - add up the value of the hand, and subtract it from the toal.
5. Maneki-neko in your hand will eat another card in your hand if there's any available, eat the highest valued remaing card.
